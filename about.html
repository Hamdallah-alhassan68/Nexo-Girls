    About Nexo Girls

    Nexo Girls is a youth-led initiative under the Climiradi Roberts Foundation, a registered nonprofit in Nigeria, that promotes young women's participation in STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics) by equipping them with globally in-demand skills to bridge the gender gap, alleviate poverty, and challenge harmful stereotypes.

    Through four programmatic core sectors— STEAM education, mentorship & leadership, social advocacy, and economic opportunities — we work to transform gendered barriers into ladders of opportunity. We believe in the power of technology and education to transform lives, ensuring a future where every girl thrives.

    Within 3 years of operations, we have impacted over 70,000 youths across Africa and beyond through digital skills programs, storytelling labs, mentorship, and strategic partnerships. While we operate in Nigeria, Rwanda & Kenya, our programs and online platforms continue to engage girls from other regions, expanding our reach through school tours, training sessions, global awareness campaigns, etc.

    The Problems We Address

    Nexo Girls is committed to addressing critical challenges affecting girls and young women across Africa through education, mentorship, innovation, and opportunity.

    Socioeconomic Challenges

    Poverty and economic limitations reduce opportunities for girls to grow, learn, and build sustainable futures.

    Education Gaps

    Unequal access to quality education continues to prevent many girls from reaching their academic and career goals.

    Gender Inequality in STE(A)M

    Many girls still face barriers in accessing opportunities and representation in science, technology, engineering, arts, and mathematics fields.

    Partnering With Other Organization to Achieve Our Goal

    Young women across Africa often lack access to digital education, technology tools, and practical tech training.

    Why Nexo Girls?

    At Nexo Girls, we believe every girl deserves the opportunity to dream, grow, and lead with confidence. We are committed to creating a supportive space where young girls are empowered through education, mentorship, technology, and leadership opportunities that help them unlock their full potential and create lasting impact in their communities.

    Our Vision

    We envision a world where every girl has access to education, digital literacy, and opportunities, enabling her to drive positive change and create a more inclusive and equitable society

    Our Mission

    To empower young girls to unlock their full potential and become catalysts for the change through comprehensive education, digital literacy programs, and strategic partnerships

    Our Core Programmatic Sectors'

    STEAM Education

    Promoting young women's participation and equipping them with globally in-demand skills in Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics.

    Mentorship & Leadership

    Fostering a generation of confident, empowered female leaders through peer-to-peer support, our "Big Sister Circle" and professional guidance.

    Social Advocacy

    Running global social impact campaigns to challenge harmful stereotypes and combat issues like gender-based violence. We actively focus on the intersection of technology and safety, leveraging digital platforms for empowerment.

    Economic Opportunities

    Providing entrepreneurship support, skills training, and scholarships to help alleviate poverty and create financial independence.

    Our Core Value

    What binds Nexo Girls

    Empowerment

    We believe girls should not just be passive consumers of technology. We equip them with the STEAM and digital skills to reach their full potential and become active creators of their own economic liberation and climate resilience.

    Inclusion & Safety

    We recognize that safety is a prerequisite for learning. We create secure, inclusive spaces, both online and offline, where every girl can learn fearlessly, free from gender-based violence and harmful stereotypes.

    Community & Leadership

    We nurture bold changemakers through a pan-African sisterhood and a "girls teach girls" model. We believe in the power of peer mentorship, where our beneficiaries return to lead, train, and guide the next generation.

    Innovation

    We encourage forward-thinking digital skills by ensuring every tech and climate solution we build is not just theoretical but rooted in the lived experiences, stories, and direct needs of our local communities.
